The story

Engineering taught me precision. Building taught me empathy.

I am pursuing B.Tech in Aeronautical Engineering at Nalanda College of Engineering, Chandi. Outside the classroom, I build products around problems I can observe—lost assets, farm uncertainty, fragmented business records and inaccessible digital workflows.

My strongest work lives at the intersection of product thinking, rapid execution and local context.

“Useful innovation begins when technology understands the person using it.”
